Energy load schedule · consumption · bill · savings

How the numbers are calculated

Same formulas as the workbook Energy Load Schedule & Consumption Calculator (version 2026-11-19). The sample project reproduces every output of that workbook; the automated tests check this.

  1. Rated kW/unit kW × 1, HP × 0.7457, W × 0.001. No motor efficiency is applied to HP.
  2. Connected load Qty × rated kW. Demand load Connected × load factor × diversity factor.
  3. Daily kWh Qty × kW × LF × hours/day. Diversity affects kW demand only, not energy.
  4. Annual kWh Daily × days/week × 52 × months/12. Months in use start at the start month and wrap the year. A fractional month counts in part (7.5 months from October = Oct–Apr full, May half).
  5. Energy rate: flat tariff, or with time-of-use (peak h × peak rate + off-peak h × off-peak rate) / hours, where peak hours are the overlap of [start, start + hours) with the peak window, wrapping past midnight.
  6. Monthly max demand is the sum of demand kW of every item in use that month. It is a coincident estimate, not a metered 15/30-minute peak. Billed demand is that kW, or kW ÷ site PF on a kVA basis.
  7. Bill (energy + demand charge + fixed + reactive penalty) × (1 + tax). Excess kVArh kWh × max(0, tanφ(site PF) − tanφ(PF limit)).
  8. Savings new kWh uses the new kW, hours or LF (blank keeps current). Cost saved uses the row's energy rate only. NPV = PV(rate, life, saving) − investment; IRR is the rate where NPV = 0.
  9. Calibration (model − meter) / meter per month, flagged above the tolerance (default ±15%).

Assumptions you must confirm

  • Tariff 230 MNT/kWh, TOU 290/150, demand charge 10,000 MNT/kW-month, site PF 0.90, CO₂ 1.0 kg/kWh and discount rate 8% are placeholders, not published values. Copy real values from a bill.
  • Demand includes the load factor. For loads that cycle (compressors, pumps on/off) the real 15/30-minute peak is closer to connected × diversity, so the demand charge here can be low. Check against a meter.
  • 52 weeks a year; holidays are not deducted. All months are 1/12 of the year.
  • Measures on the same row are not interactive: two measures on one ID each count against the original row.
  • Savings exclude demand-charge, reactive-penalty and tax effects, and assume a constant real saving every year.
  • Mongolian labels are machine-assisted and need checking by a native speaker.

An engineering estimate for budgeting and screening, not an investment-grade audit (ASHRAE Level 2/3 or ISO 50002).
